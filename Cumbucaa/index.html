An ode to Brazil’s textures, flavors and creativity.

A full exploration on the length of what the tropics has to offer you. Cumbuca is the celebration of the vast cuisine of Brazil, from north to south, deliberately crafted to remind you of the powerful and nostalgia-packed flavors of South America.

Cumbuca { kuîmbuka } stems from the indigenous language tupi, one of the many originary groups that live in the richest parts of Brazil, and it means "bowl to share". It is a symbol that carries nurture, flavor, and most importantly, the ritual of sharing, something extremely common in any Brazilian meal.
